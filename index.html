Common Questions

What is the shelf life of your products?+
Oils: 12 months from press date. Powders: 18 months. Store in a cool, dark place away from direct sunlight. We ship fresh within 48 hours of pressing, so your product is as close to extraction date as possible.
Are your oils safe for high-heat Indian cooking?+
Yes. Mustard Oil has a smoke point of ~250°C and Groundnut Oil ~230°C — both ideal for deep frying, tadka, and everyday high-heat cooking. They are stable oils that don't break down easily.
क्या निरामिश उत्पाद 100% शुद्ध हैं? / Are products truly pure?+
Absolutely. Every batch is tested at NABL-accredited labs. Our products contain exactly one ingredient — what's on the label. Nothing added, nothing removed. You can request any batch's lab report by contacting us on WhatsApp.
How do I use Moringa Powder daily?+
Start with ½ teaspoon per day in warm water, a smoothie, or your morning dal. Increase gradually to 1 teaspoon. It blends well into soups, chutneys, and even flatbread dough for a nutrient boost with a mild, earthy flavour.
Do you offer Cash on Delivery (COD)?+
Yes! We offer UPI, Debit/Credit Card, Net Banking, and Cash on Delivery across most pin codes in India. Free delivery on orders above ₹499. Delivery typically takes 3–5 working days.
What is your return & refund policy?+
Every Niramish product has a 30-day satisfaction guarantee. If you're not completely happy for any reason — quality, freshness, or otherwise — contact us and we will arrange a full refund. No hoops, no hassle.
